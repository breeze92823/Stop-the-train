## The game
Vehicles are grabbed from the Vehicle Yard in the south and parked on the east-west railway in the north. A train comes out of the east tunnel and hits them: each vehicle takes its HP off the train. Train at 0 HP: it derails and pays its reward plus the coins of that pass's hits, then a new train is picked. Train gets through: no reward, only `ROUND.throughShare` of that pass's hit coins drop by the rail (`sound.trainLost`), and a new random train is sent (the damaged one does not come back). A vehicle's bay number is both the Strength needed to lift it and its HP (times the variant: GOLDEN x1.5, EMERALD x6). Strength is the `power` stat, and it comes from the gym in the east: hold E at a bench to lie down, each half rep adds `baseGain x bench mult x multiplier x Training upgrade`, until STOP TRAINING (the button, E or jump). Benches unlock by rebirth count; the ADMIN bench is sold for coins. A rebirth needs Strength (`rebirthCost()`), sets it back to `REBIRTH.startPower` and keeps everything else: +1 multiplier, +`REBIRTH.luck`% luck. Interact = hold E for `YARD.holdSeconds` (2 s), or hold a finger on the prompt. The rail and the train are shared: with the server, ONE round per room (backend `round.ts`: clock, parked vehicles, train position and HP) and `shared.js` mirrors it (`park`/`seedRail` out; `round`, `railAdd`, `railRefuse`, `trainTick`, `hit`, `derail`, `gone` in), so every player's vehicles stack against the same train; coins from a hit go to the vehicle's owner (held until the derail), the derail reward split by the damage each player did (`pay` in `derail`). Offline the local round in `game.js` runs the same rules for one player.

**Fair trains** (`fair.js`, tuned by `FAIR` in config.js, mirroring the backend `fair.ts`/`FAIR`): no fixed train HP. A train is sized to the room's power (each player's best liftable vehicle x Carry level; `net.js` sends `stats {power, carry}` when that changes) x `targetRatio`, then x the ladder and a random spread; the nearest tier (`TRAINS`) only gives its name and look, and its reward follows the tiers on a log-log line, so `g.maxHp`/`g.reward` (not `spec.hp`/`spec.reward`) are the real numbers. A stopped train climbs the ladder (`ladder.win`), a lost one drops it (`ladder.loss`), settling rooms near 60% stopped; wins in a row pay a streak bonus, `comebackAfter` losses bring an X2 COMEBACK train. Online only: damage cap while teammates have vehicles in the pass, reward split with a floor, overkill cut, underdog +50%, room milestones. The HUD shows the ladder as chips under the timer (`hud.setRoundInfo`), a near-miss banner and a NEXT STEP hint (`game.js nextStep`) after a loss, a GOALS panel (`goals.js`: three session goals, not saved, bigger each set) and the TRAIN BOOK window (`progress.trainsStopped`, saved; the first stop of a tier pays the reward again). The Carry upgrade level (Day 4 reward: +1) is how many vehicles are stacked in the hands (`yard.js`): the first grabbed rests on the palms (`animator.handAnchor` = midpoint of both palms from the arm bones, `YARD.handSink`/`handReach`), the rest sit on it, each extra one slows the walk (`YARD.carrySlow`). E parks / drops (Q, G) / inserts into the Fuse Machine the top one; at capacity the bay prompt says Full.

Windows (hud.js): UPGRADES! (`UPGRADES` in config: Magnet, Walkspeed, Cooldown and Carry are wired, Fuse CD is saved only), DAILY REWARDS! (`DAILY`, a 7 day cycle, Day 7 = permanent x5 Strength gain via `state.powerMult`), FUSE CONFIRMATION. Fuse Machine (`fuse.js`, `FUSE`): carry a vehicle to its pad, hold E to insert, three in -> the window; paying `FUSE.cost` coins succeeds with the Luck % and puts one golden/emerald vehicle with the summed HP on the rail, else the three are lost. Everything is bought with coins (no Robux): upgrades, the Store (`SHOP.power2` x2 Power, the Admin bench; one-time buys saved as `levels[id] = 1`, `progress.buyShop`) and Skip Rebirth (`REBIRTH.skipCoins`, still needs the full Strength, keeps it).

West of the plaza: the Upgrade stall, Fuse Machine, Galaxy World portal, Feedback mailbox (walking in opens the portal menu, like Esc) and three leaderboards (the server's top ten, the local player alone offline). North-east of the plaza: the FREE COINS chest (hold E; opens after `FREE_COINS.playSeconds` = 30 min of play, counted by the server, countdown above the chest; claim goes through `claimChestOnServer` in `net.js`, paying `FREE_COINS.value` = 10K; offline it runs on a local clock).

**Galaxy World**: at `WORLDS.galaxy.rebirths` (36) the portal takes the player to a second world: the same game (same map, colliders, trains, benches, yard, progress) in another look, with its own rail and train and only the players in it drawn. Walking into the portal ring travels (`worlds.js`: the gate, the warp, `trains.abort`, `rail.reset`, `game.restart`, then the plaza; what is carried comes along); in the Galaxy the same portal leads back. Nothing is rebuilt: surfaces register with `theme.js` under a key (`themeMaterial`, `themeColors` for vertex colours, `onTheme` for anything else) and a world's `looks` in `WORLDS` (config.js) overrides that key; what a look paints is made on first entry, behind the warp, and kept. `lighting.setMoods` takes the world's sky (its `nebula` is painted once by `paintNebula` and added to the sky shader), `world.js` hides the clouds and shows the planets and asteroid belt, `lobby.js` the stardust and the portal's labels and colours, `audio/index.js` `setAmbience` the drone and glints instead of the breeze and birds. Each page load starts in the main world (the world is not saved). Online it is all one room: `net.js` `sendWorld` (`setWorld`), `net.world` filters `remotePlayers.js`, and `shared.js` ignores `round`/`gone` from another world; the server can answer `worldRefused`, which sends the player home (`worlds.js`). Keep `WORLDS[...].rebirths` equal to the backend's.

## Files (`src/`)
- `main.js`: boot order = Bloxity SDK -> renderer -> Rapier -> lobby, fx, hud, rail, yard, game, trains -> avatar -> loop (`frame(real, elapsed)`). `game.g.timeScale` slows the sim during a derail. `governQuality` steps the preset down while frames stay slow, and Intel/integrated GPUs start on Medium. Dev-only `__game` exposes the systems plus `advance(seconds, step)` (steps the loop by hand; rAF does not run in a hidden preview pane) and `bench(n)` (true ms per frame), `teleport(x, y, z, facing)` (facing `Math.PI` = north, -Z, 0 = south), `progress` (the save: set `progress.rebirths = 36` to reach the Galaxy), `worlds.go(id)`, `currentWorld()`, `remotes`, `net`. Testing against a local backend: the root `.claude/launch.json` has `stop-the-train-backend` and `stop-the-train-net` on their own ports (2571, 5196) because other games' servers hold the defaults; check what owns a port before trusting a result.
- **Game loop**: `game.js` rounds and rewards (its `hooks` drive `trains.js`; online `trains.js` leaves `onDerail` to `shared.js`, which passes the server's pay). `fair.js` train sizing and pay math. `goals.js` session goals. `trains.js` procedural diesels and wagons run as kinematic bodies, so they shove parked vehicles; at 0 HP each car turns dynamic, held together by `couple()` (impulses at the couplers, never a Rapier joint: 0.14 hits `unreachable` on the step after removing a body that ever had one, and the world stays dead). `rail.js` vehicles out in the world as dynamic bodies: thrown on a ballistic arc, stacking, `smash` (crumple + launch), reclaimed after `SPENT_LIFE`. `yard.js` bays, labels, hold-E, the lift, carrying, parking, drop, hologram + respawn; it also writes `player.speedScale` and owns the prompt (other systems offer targets through its `extra` callback: `{ kind, bay, x, y, z, text, act, instant }`). `gym.js` benches (frames in one merged vertex-coloured mesh, a mesh per barbell), training, `placeAvatar` (lays the player group on the pad while `player.locked` holds the capsule beside it), auras. `coins.js` collectible coins. `progress.js` save, upgrades, rebirth, daily, `compact()`.
- **Vehicles**: `vehicles.js`. One merged geometry and one material per model: colours in vertex colours, roughness/metal/glow from a lookup texture addressed by UV ("slots"). Cars are lofted from side profiles (`STYLES`). `crumple` dents a private copy of the geometry. `variantMaterial` is the flowing golden/emerald paint, `lockedMaterial` the dark silhouette. Trains use the same `Kit`.
- **Effects**: `fx.js` additive + alpha instanced billboard pools with HDR colour ramps (the `n...` ramps are for the alpha pool: on the bright lawn additive colour turns yellow-green), debris, flash lights, rings, decals, emitters (`addEmitter({ rate, tick })`). **HUD**: `hud.js` + `hud.css`, DOM over the canvas, sized from `--u`; the Rebirth window is its own layout (`.hud-rb`, sized from `--r`) and blurs the canvas instead of dimming the HUD. **Audio**: `audio/index.js` everything synthesised; world sounds take x, z and are panned from the camera; `trainTick` is the live engine with Doppler.
- **Player**: `player.js` kinematic capsule + Rapier `KinematicCharacterController` (autostep, slopes, snap-to-ground, pushes dynamic bodies), own gravity/jump (coyote time + buffer). Feet position is `player.position`; `player.renderPosition` is interpolated for drawing. Falling below `PHYSICS.killY` respawns. `camera.js` third-person chase cam (boom, wheel zoom, wall clearance via `segmentClear`, speed FOV) and first person at the inner zoom end; `modes.js` `character` (avatar visible) / `player` (first person, avatar hidden), V toggles. `input.js` keyboard / mouse / pointer lock into `inputState`; `touch.js` + `touch.css` touch controls.
- `avatar/`: `character.js` (player.glb or procedural fallback), `loader.js` (Bloxity cosmetics + proportions from the CDN, `cdn.js`), `rig.js` + `animator.js` (procedural idle/run/airborne/landing; tunables in `ANIM`).
- **Physics**: `physics.js` Rapier world, fixed 60 Hz step with an accumulator; dynamic meshes bound via `bindMesh`/`addDynamic` are interpolated between ticks. Static geometry uses `addFixed` / `addFixedBoxFor`. `segmentClear` is the camera's ray against static colliders only.
- **Ground**: `world.js` the lawn, voxel terraces (`terraceLayers`, mesher in `voxels.js`), tunnel mountains, rails, chevrons, plaza, yard lines and `landColliders()`. `lobby.js` everything standing on it (stall + NPC, Fuse Machine, mailbox, portal, chest, boards, palms, trees, floating signs, the flat HP readout) and the West effects; static groups go through `settle()`/`bake()`. Big surfaces are Lambert on purpose: they cover most pixels, and PBR there cost the integrated GPUs their frame rate.
- **Sky and light**: `lighting.js` sky shader, stars, PMREM environment, shadowed sun following the player, fog, day/night blend (N, each world has its own pair of moods; `setMoods(sky)` swaps them). `addLamp` is an unused hook. `postfx.js` bloom (EffectComposer: HDR half-float render -> UnrealBloomPass -> OutputPass), on for the High/Ultra presets only (`BLOOM`; threshold 2.0 so sunlit surfaces never bloom).
- **Materials and textures**: `textures.js` canvas painters (cracked sand, water ripples, grass/tiles/crates/planks, outlined text, soft dots, sparkles, ...) and `toTexture` / `mulberry32`. `propkit.js` shared prop helpers: `std`, `glow`, `neon(color, k)` (unlit, HDR-bright with bloom; switched by `setNeonBloom`), `put`, `solidBox` / `solidPole` colliders, `label` (outlined text sprite, repainted once Fredoka loads). `bake.js` merges a Group's static meshes to one mesh per material (draw-call saver; unused so far).
- Controls: WASD/arrows, Shift sprint, Space jump (or stop training), hold E to interact, Q or G to drop, click the world to look (pointer lock, as in Climb-and-slide-for-eggs; Esc releases it, and opening a HUD window or starting a bench exits the lock so buttons can be pressed; drag-to-look is the fallback where the lock is refused), Esc opens the portal menu, wheel zoom, V mode, N day/night, R respawn. Never bind P (CrazyGames: Boxity's pause menu).
- Graphics: `graphicsPreset(name)` in config resolves the preset; on coarse pointers the default is Medium and every preset is capped by `MOBILE_GRAPHICS_CAP` (DPR 1.5, 1024 shadow map, no bloom), because the Bloxity SDK sends `High` itself.
- Boxity layout (required to publish, docs.bloxity.io Platform UI rules): Boxity draws the menu / pause menu, chat, emotes, avatar editor, invite, leaderboard and sign-in itself in a toolbar in the top-left corner with the chat feed under it, so the game never draws those buttons and keeps any HUD out of those rectangles (`bloxity.onReservedAreas`).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
