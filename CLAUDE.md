## The game
Vehicles are grabbed from the Vehicle Yard in the south and parked on the east-west railway in the north. A train comes out of the east tunnel and hits them: each vehicle takes its HP off the train. Train at 0 HP: it derails and pays its reward plus the coins of that pass's hits, then a new train is picked. Train gets through: nothing is paid, that pass's hit coins are lost (`sound.trainLost`), and it keeps its damage and comes back ("TRAIN COMING BACK IN"). A vehicle's bay number is both the Strength needed to lift it and its HP (times the variant: GOLDEN x1.5, EMERALD x6). Strength is the `power` stat, and it comes from the gym in the east: hold E at a bench to lie down, each half rep adds `baseGain x bench mult x multiplier x Training upgrade`, until STOP TRAINING (the button, E or jump). Benches unlock by rebirth count; the ADMIN bench is sold for coins. A rebirth needs Strength (`rebirthCost()`), sets it back to `REBIRTH.startPower` and keeps everything else: +1 multiplier, +`REBIRTH.luck`% luck. Interact = hold E for `YARD.holdSeconds` (2 s), or hold a finger on the prompt. The rail and the train are shared: with the server, ONE round per room (backend `round.ts`: clock, parked vehicles, train position and HP) and `shared.js` mirrors it (`park`/`seedRail` out; `round`, `railAdd`, `railRefuse`, `trainTick`, `hit`, `derail`, `gone` in), so every player's vehicles stack against the same train; coins from a hit go to the vehicle's owner (held until the derail), the derail reward to everyone who had a vehicle hit. Offline the local round in `game.js` runs unchanged. The Carry upgrade level (Day 4 reward: +1) is how many vehicles are stacked in the hands (`yard.js`): the first grabbed rests on the palms (`animator.handAnchor` = midpoint of both palms from the arm bones, `YARD.handSink`/`handReach`), the rest sit on it, each extra one slows the walk (`YARD.carrySlow`). E parks / drops (Q, G) / inserts into the Fuse Machine the top one; at capacity the bay prompt says Full.

Windows (hud.js): UPGRADES! (`UPGRADES` in config: Magnet, Walkspeed, Cooldown and Carry are wired, Fuse CD is saved only), DAILY REWARDS! (`DAILY`, a 7 day cycle, Day 7 = permanent x5 Strength gain via `state.powerMult`), FUSE CONFIRMATION. Fuse Machine (`fuse.js`, `FUSE`): carry a vehicle to its pad, hold E to insert, three in -> the window; paying `FUSE.cost` coins succeeds with the Luck % and puts one golden/emerald vehicle with the summed HP on the rail, else the three are lost. Everything is bought with coins (no Robux): upgrades, the Store (`SHOP.power2` x2 Power, the Admin bench; one-time buys saved as `levels[id] = 1`, `progress.buyShop`) and Skip Rebirth (`REBIRTH.skipCoins`, still needs the full Strength, keeps it).

West of the plaza: the Upgrade stall, Fuse Machine, Galaxy World portal, Feedback mailbox (walking in opens the portal menu, like Esc) and three leaderboards (the server's top ten, the local player alone offline). North-east of the plaza: the FREE COINS chest (hold E; opens after `FREE_COINS.playSeconds` = 30 min of play, counted by the server, countdown above the chest; claim goes through `claimChestOnServer` in `net.js`, paying `FREE_COINS.value` = 10K; offline it runs on a local clock).

Not built yet: what the Fuse Machine does, Galaxy World, Gems purchases (deliberately skipped).

Three.js (vanilla, no React) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka (loaded in `index.html`).

- `npm run dev` / `npm run build`. `VITE_DEV_MODE=true` (see `.env.example`) skips the Bloxity SDK/CDN. Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` uploads `dist/` to Bloxity Hosting (main = prod, dev = dev channel).
- All tunables are in `src/config.js` (the rest of `src/` holds no theme values): identity, `LOBBY`, `TRACK`, `SET` (where set pieces stand), `VARIANTS`, `YARD` (bays and their requirements), `GYM` (benches, reps, the auras' colours and rates), `FREE_COINS`, `ROUND`, `TRAINS`, `PROGRESS`, `UPGRADES`, `REBIRTH`, `COINS`, `FX`, `AUDIO`, plus physics, camera, look, presets, touch and the rig (`ANIM` includes the `grip` / `carry` / `toss` overlays and the `bench` press).
- All Bloxity SDK calls go through `src/bloxity.js` and must never throw (SDK may be blocked). It handles init/loading steps, auth (user/guest), settings (`graphics_quality`, `camera_sensitivity`, `show_fps`, `fullscreen`), avatar + proportion change events, the portal `respawn_request` event, the avatar customizer, the matchmaker helpers `net.js` uses (`resolveServerEndpoint`, `locateRoom`, `updateRoom`), the game token, emotes (`registerFeature('emotes')`, `play_emote`, catalogue), chat bubbles, rewarded and screen ads (+ ad-break pause/mute), the death screen, leaderboard `define` (`BOARDS` in config.js, mirrored by the backend which reports scores) and `registerFeature('play_with_friends')`. Not implemented on purpose: Gems.

## Files (`src/`)
- `main.js`: boot order = Bloxity SDK -> renderer -> Rapier -> lobby, fx, hud, rail, yard, game, trains -> avatar -> loop (`frame(real, elapsed)`). `game.g.timeScale` slows the sim during a derail. `governQuality` steps the preset down while frames stay slow, and Intel/integrated GPUs start on Medium. Dev-only `__game` exposes the systems plus `advance(seconds, step)` (steps the loop by hand; rAF does not run in a hidden preview pane) and `bench(n)` (true ms per frame), `teleport(x, y, z, facing)` (facing `Math.PI` = north, -Z, 0 = south).
- **Game loop**: `game.js` rounds and rewards (its `hooks` drive `trains.js`). `trains.js` procedural diesels and wagons run as kinematic bodies, so they shove parked vehicles; at 0 HP each car turns dynamic, coupled with spherical joints. `rail.js` vehicles out in the world as dynamic bodies: thrown on a ballistic arc, stacking, `smash` (crumple + launch), reclaimed after `SPENT_LIFE`. `yard.js` bays, labels, hold-E, the lift, carrying, parking, drop, hologram + respawn; it also writes `player.speedScale` and owns the prompt (other systems offer targets through its `extra` callback: `{ kind, bay, x, y, z, text, act, instant }`). `gym.js` benches (frames in one merged vertex-coloured mesh, a mesh per barbell), training, `placeAvatar` (lays the player group on the pad while `player.locked` holds the capsule beside it), auras. `coins.js` collectible coins. `progress.js` save, upgrades, rebirth, daily, `compact()`.
- **Vehicles**: `vehicles.js`. One merged geometry and one material per model: colours in vertex colours, roughness/metal/glow from a lookup texture addressed by UV ("slots"). Cars are lofted from side profiles (`STYLES`). `crumple` dents a private copy of the geometry. `variantMaterial` is the flowing golden/emerald paint, `lockedMaterial` the dark silhouette. Trains use the same `Kit`.
- **Effects**: `fx.js` additive + alpha instanced billboard pools with HDR colour ramps (the `n...` ramps are for the alpha pool: on the bright lawn additive colour turns yellow-green), debris, flash lights, rings, decals, emitters (`addEmitter({ rate, tick })`). **HUD**: `hud.js` + `hud.css`, DOM over the canvas, sized from `--u`; the Rebirth window is its own layout (`.hud-rb`, sized from `--r`) and blurs the canvas instead of dimming the HUD. **Audio**: `audio/index.js` everything synthesised; world sounds take x, z and are panned from the camera; `trainTick` is the live engine with Doppler.
- **Player**: `player.js` kinematic capsule + Rapier `KinematicCharacterController` (autostep, slopes, snap-to-ground, pushes dynamic bodies), own gravity/jump (coyote time + buffer). Feet position is `player.position`; `player.renderPosition` is interpolated for drawing. Falling below `PHYSICS.killY` respawns. `camera.js` third-person chase cam (boom, wheel zoom, wall clearance via `segmentClear`, speed FOV) and first person at the inner zoom end; `modes.js` `character` (avatar visible) / `player` (first person, avatar hidden), V toggles. `input.js` keyboard / mouse / pointer lock into `inputState`; `touch.js` + `touch.css` touch controls.
- `avatar/`: `character.js` (player.glb or procedural fallback), `loader.js` (Bloxity cosmetics + proportions from the CDN, `cdn.js`), `rig.js` + `animator.js` (procedural idle/run/airborne/landing; tunables in `ANIM`).
- **Physics**: `physics.js` Rapier world, fixed 60 Hz step with an accumulator; dynamic meshes bound via `bindMesh`/`addDynamic` are interpolated between ticks. Static geometry uses `addFixed` / `addFixedBoxFor`. `segmentClear` is the camera's ray against static colliders only.
- **Ground**: `world.js` the lawn, voxel terraces (`terraceLayers`, mesher in `voxels.js`), tunnel mountains, rails, chevrons, plaza, yard lines and `landColliders()`. `lobby.js` everything standing on it (stall + NPC, Fuse Machine, mailbox, portal, chest, boards, palms, trees, floating signs, the flat HP readout) and the West effects; static groups go through `settle()`/`bake()`. Big surfaces are Lambert on purpose: they cover most pixels, and PBR there cost the integrated GPUs their frame rate.
- **Sky and light**: `lighting.js` sky shader, stars, PMREM environment, shadowed sun following the player, fog, day/night blend (N). `addLamp` and `setMoods(sky)` are unused hooks. `postfx.js` bloom (EffectComposer: HDR half-float render -> UnrealBloomPass -> OutputPass), on for the High/Ultra presets only (`BLOOM`; threshold 2.0 so sunlit surfaces never bloom).
- **Materials and textures**: `textures.js` canvas painters (cracked sand, water ripples, grass/tiles/crates/planks, outlined text, soft dots, sparkles, ...) and `toTexture` / `mulberry32`. `propkit.js` shared prop helpers: `std`, `glow`, `neon(color, k)` (unlit, HDR-bright with bloom; switched by `setNeonBloom`), `put`, `solidBox` / `solidPole` colliders, `label` (outlined text sprite, repainted once Fredoka loads). `bake.js` merges a Group's static meshes to one mesh per material (draw-call saver; unused so far).
- Controls: WASD/arrows, Shift sprint, Space jump (or stop training), hold E to interact, Q or G to drop, drag to look (no pointer lock: the cursor stays free for the HUD, as in the reference), Esc opens the portal menu, wheel zoom, V mode, N day/night, R respawn. Never bind P (CrazyGames: Boxity's pause menu).
- Graphics: `graphicsPreset(name)` in config resolves the preset; on coarse pointers the default is Medium and every preset is capped by `MOBILE_GRAPHICS_CAP` (DPR 1.5, 1024 shadow map, no bloom), because the Bloxity SDK sends `High` itself.
- Boxity layout (required to publish, docs.bloxity.io Platform UI rules): Boxity draws the menu / pause menu, chat, emotes, avatar editor, invite, leaderboard and sign-in itself in a toolbar in the top-left corner with the chat feed under it, so the game never draws those buttons and keeps any HUD out of those rectangles (`bloxity.onReservedAreas`).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
